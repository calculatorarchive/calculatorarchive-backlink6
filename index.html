Frequently Asked Questions About Tipping

How do I calculate a 15 percent tip?

To calculate a 15 percent tip, multiply your bill amount by 0.15. For example, a $50 bill would have a $7.50 tip, making the total $57.50. Use our free tip calculator above for instant results.

What is a good tip percentage for restaurant service?

Standard restaurant tipping in the US is 15-20 percent of the pre-tax bill amount. 15 percent for average service, 18 percent for good service, and 20 percent or more for excellent service. Many restaurants now suggest tip percentages on receipts.

Should I tip on the total or subtotal?

You should typically tip on the pre-tax subtotal. However, many people tip on the total including tax for convenience. The difference is usually minimal, around $1-2 on a $100 bill. Our tip calculator lets you choose which amount to use.

How much should I tip for takeout orders?

For takeout orders, tipping is optional but appreciated. A small tip of 5-10 percent is common for larger orders or exceptional service. Some people round up to the nearest dollar for small orders.

Is 20 percent tip standard?

A 20 percent tip has become increasingly standard in many parts of the US, especially for sit-down restaurant service. It shows appreciation for good service and helps support service workers who often rely on tips as a significant part of their income.
